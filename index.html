PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Zyntra AI ("we", "our", "the app") is a personal AI assistant developed by Ghanchi Moin. This policy explains what information Zyntra AI collects, how it is used, and your choices.

Information we collect

When you use Zyntra AI, the messages you send to AI Chat, Study Helper, Business Tools and Voice Assistant, along with the prompts and any reference images you provide to Image Generator, are sent to our AI provider to generate a response. If you sign in, the email you enter is stored locally in your browser. If you submit the Contact form, we receive the name, email, and message you provide.

Where your data is processed

Chat, Study Helper, Business Tools and Voice Assistant replies are generated using Groq's AI API. Images are generated using Pollinations AI. These third-party providers process your prompts to generate a response; we do not control their servers.

Local storage

Zyntra AI stores your sign-in state, chat history, and usage stats directly in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us unless you submit it through the Contact or Report feature.

AI-generated content

Responses and images are generated by AI and may sometimes be inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate. You can flag any AI output using the Report button, which sends the flagged content to us via the Contact form.

Children's privacy

Zyntra AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can clear your chat history at any time from the sidebar, and you can sign out at any time from your profile. Clearing your browser data will remove all locally stored information.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact Ghanchi Moin using the Contact form on this site.
